BIOTURE GROUP
Operational Divisions & Companies

1. BioTure Real Estate
Redefining housing from property acquisition through to property development, with state-of-the-art designs.

2. BioTure Commerce
Sourcing, presenting and delivering goods across selected consumer categories.
- Beverages: enhancing health through carefully brewed recipes for unique-taste beverages.
- Fashion: reshaping culture and redefining fashion trends, blending contemporary and modern fusions.

3. BioTure Agriculture & Food
Agricultural development and food processing capabilities that strengthen the value chain.
- Agriculture: from animal husbandry to plant cultivation and the integration of technology.
- Food processing: transforming agricultural yields into ready-to-consumer products.

4. BioTure Resources & Energy
Responsible participation across mineral resources and the oil and gas value chain.
- Mining & Minerals: extracting Cameroon's rich variety of mineral resources, such as bauxite, copper and gold.
- Oil and gas: drilling into and extracting oil deposits and refining for consumer markets.

5. BioTure Manufacturing
Creativity and bespoke engineering behind manufactured products and machines.
- Manufacturing: creativity and bespoke engineering, co-manufacturing and unique innovation of products and machines.
- Designing and developing gadgets, integrating artificial intelligence across sectors to enhance efficiencies.

6. BioTure Health & Wellness
Enhancing wellness through medicine, research and personal care.
- Biomedicals and pharmaceuticals: enhancing wellness through genetic research, disease control and improvements in medicine.
- Cosmetics: crafting unique skin care products, fragrances and beauty enhancement services.

7. BioTure Transport & Logistics
Connecting people and goods across local, regional and international routes.
- Transport and Logistics: connecting people and goods across a wide transport network.
- Aviation and transport transit: connecting local and international flights, reducing wait time.
- Automobile: engineering affordable and eco-friendly automobiles.

8. BioTure Finance & Consultancy
A shared platform for finance, insurance and consultancy services.
- Finance: simplifying banking and making finance easily accessible to individuals; providing financial intelligence for the best family, business and economic decisions.
- Insurance: providing a safety net for individuals, businesses and institutions.
- Consultancy: across family, business, leadership and governance.
